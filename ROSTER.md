# Digital Football — character roster

Stats are SPD (speed) · POW (kick power) · STA (stamina) · CRL (curl) ·
ACC (accuracy) · TCK (tackling). See `client/src/characters.ts` for the pips
and `spacetimedb/src/index.ts` `CHAR_STATS` for the real multipliers — same
order in both files. All characters are selectable today; the wacky roster is
designed to become unlockables later.
